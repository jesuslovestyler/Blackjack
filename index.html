Six-deck shoe, reshuffled when about three-quarters is dealt. The dealer keeps one card face-down until you finish, hits until 17, and stands on all 17s. Blackjack pays 3 to 2. A tie (push) returns your bet. Double takes exactly one more card; split pairs play as two hands, and split aces get one card each.

Keys: H hit · S stand · D double · Space deal / new hand
